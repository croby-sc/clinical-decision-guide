Welcome to the Clinical Test Decision Guide

The purpose of this site is to provide a simple, easy-to-navigate clinical decision guide that helps medical providers quickly determine which diagnostic test is most appropriate based on the patient’s symptoms. This tool streamlines decision-making for ordering tests, reduces unnecessary testing, and improves workflow efficiency by giving providers a clear, organized reference tool.
